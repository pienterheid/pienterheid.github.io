Privacy policy

Hermes health bridge is a private tool used by one person. It accesses only that person's own WHOOP account, through WHOOP's official API, in order to read recovery, sleep, cycle (strain), workout and body measurement data. It does not access the WHOOP profile.

The data is stored on the owner's own server and used only for that person's own questions. When he asks his assistant about it, summaries of the relevant records are sent to the assistant's AI model provider to answer that question. It is not sold, not shared with third parties for their own use, not used for advertising, and not used by this tool to train any AI model.

Access can be withdrawn at any time by revoking the app's access in the WHOOP account.

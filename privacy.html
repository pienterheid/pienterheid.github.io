Privacy policy

Hermes calendar bridge is a private tool used by one person. It accesses only the Google calendars that have been explicitly shared with its Google account, in order to read events and add or change events where that has been allowed.

Calendar data is used only for that purpose. It is not sold, not shared with third parties for their own use, and not used for advertising.

Access can be withdrawn at any time by removing the calendar's sharing or revoking the app's access in the Google account.
